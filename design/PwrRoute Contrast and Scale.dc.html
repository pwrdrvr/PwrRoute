1Dimmed content keeps its contrast (PWR-13)

Every text token already clears 4.5:1, so the failures come from .opacity() on boxes that hold text: a rule that is off, a step the link missed, a step to come, the paused map. Muted text is the floor, so it has no room to fade. One dim level survives, and it is a token swap: inside theme::dimmed primary text drops to secondary, secondary and subtle drop to muted, and colored text keeps its color. What says "off" is something other than faintness: the off chip, a strike, a dashed frame (the map's own word for "does not run", already used for a browser that is not installed), the switch. Opacity stays for what is not text (badges, icons and route lines at 0.45) and for passing states the reader caused or can end: a drag lift (0.4), the hover spotlight (0.45), a disabled control (0.5), all named in theme.

2Four palettes: dark, light, and each with Increase Contrast (PWR-15, PWR-16)

The same specimen in each palette: Overview rows (selected, at rest, off), a trace with a hit and a miss, fields with and without the focus ring, buttons, switches, a segmented control, chips and the status dots. Increase Contrast (macOS's switch, Windows high contrast) strengthens ink and edges and never the tints: every card, divider and field gets an edge you can see without its fill (the borders rise to 0.24–0.62), quiet text becomes opaque and one step brighter, the selected edge and focus ring are solid, and the status dots brighten. Raising the tints instead would lower text on them (accent on its tint falls from 7.2 to 6.2). Light is the design's light block with its gaps closed; the app draws it only as a preview (PWRROUTE_APPEARANCE=light, and the screens example's --light) until it follows the OS's appearance.

3Text scaling: View › Zoom, and layouts that reflow (PWR-14)

The model is an in-app zoom. View › Actual Size, Zoom In and Zoom Out (⌘0, ⌘+ or ⌘=, ⌘−; Ctrl elsewhere) step through 100, 110, 125, 150, 175 and 200%, kept in settings.json as view.zoom. Every window follows at once, the tray card included. macOS has no text size an AppKit app can read, so following the OS would do nothing for most of PwrRoute's users; Windows' Text size and GNOME's text scaling factor can feed the same zoom later. Zoom scales boxes with their text, as a page zooms, so nothing is cut at a fixed width; what changes is the room. A window opened zoomed starts at its design size times the zoom, as large as the display allows. The frames below are a 14-inch laptop at 200%: 756 by 472 design pixels.

4The first pass's tokens and focus rings, by eye

The first accessibility pass added --border-control (bone at 0.40) for edges that alone say "this is a field", raised --accent-border from 0.42 to 0.56 and drew the focus ring as a solid 2px accent outline, bone on a filled accent button. Seen on the screens they hold up: the field edge reads as an input without looking like a button, 0.56 is the same orange and still quieter than the ring, and the ring is unmistakable on every surface. They are now the design's values too (this stylesheet's .inp and .sw draw in --border-control). One change: with Increase Contrast the selected edge goes solid, so a lit row and a focused one stay told apart by the ring's 2px and offset, not by alpha.
