1Menu bar and tray

The router is resident and the window is optional, so the menu bar item is the surface people see most. It answers three questions without opening anything: is routing on, where did my last links go, and how do I stop routing for a minute. Pause routing sends everything to one browser until switched back; it exists because "open this one in Chrome" is the most common reason to fight a rule.
