1Settings window

Its own window, as in PwrSnap and PwrGit: 208px nav, panes of disclosure cards at most 760px wide, field rows with the label column on the left. Three panes drawn: Local agents (the MCP door, modelled on PwrSnap's and PwrGit's), Configuration (visual rules vs a Finicky config, and the importer) and Browsers (OS default registration, detected apps, profiles). General, Diagnostics and About follow the family's existing panes and are not redrawn here.

1aLocal agents. One door: loopback MCP with OAuth 2.1 and PwrRoute's own approval window. Sessions carry a role; roles are additive; Revoke is per session. Port is a placeholder (PwrSnap holds 51729).
