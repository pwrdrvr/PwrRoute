1Rules · a clear Add rule, with or without a config

With visual rules in charge, Rules has Add rule in its head. With a Finicky config in charge the head was replaced by the banner, so there was no way to add a rule at all short of Expand on a card at the bottom. The head comes back in both states.

1aRecommended. A Finicky config in charge: the same pane head as visual rules, with Add rule as the primary. The help says where a new rule goes and that the config’s rows are read-only.

3Inspector · Open in and Profile pickers

The browser scan already knows every browser and its profiles by name; the editor now offers them. Free text stays as the last row of each list, for an app or profile the scan can’t see. Lists are as wide as their field (Popovers and Overflow 1a), with the profile hint after two names.

3aRecommended. Open in lists what’s on this Mac first, then apps your rules name that the scan didn’t find (dimmed with “not installed” when nothing matches them), then Other app…. Choosing a browser clears a profile it doesn’t have.
