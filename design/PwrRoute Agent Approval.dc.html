1Approving a local agent: pick a role, then Allow

The first time an MCP client name connects, PwrRoute opens a small window and the agent's first call waits on it. As built, each role card approved the moment it was clicked and the window vanished: no selected state, no button that reads as the decision, nothing afterwards to say what happened. This turn makes the roles a radio group with the least role chosen, lists what the chosen role can and cannot do, puts the decision in Deny and Allow as <role> (Esc and Return), shows the five-minute expiry counting down, and keeps the window open on the outcome. PwrSnap and PwrGit both pick first and confirm second (radio cards and a role select, then Deny and Allow access or Approve); neither has keys, a countdown or an outcome, so PwrRoute adds those.

1aBefore, for reference. Every card is a button that approves on click; Deny sits alone at the bottom right. Nothing looks selected, so a click reads as choosing, and the window closes on it.

BehaviourWhat it does
Chosen at openReader, always: the least role, as PwrGit preselects its reader. The focus ring is on the chosen card.
Changing the roleClick a card, press 1 2 3, or Up and Down. The list and the primary button follow. A card never decides.
DecidingAllow as <role> (Return) or Deny (Esc). Closing the window while it asks denies, as before. On macOS the panel shows over the agent's terminal without taking the keyboard, so a Return typed there never reaches Allow; a click on the panel gives it the keys. Windows and Linux bring the window to the front, so there keys do nothing for its first 0.8 seconds.
CountdownFrom the prompt's time to live (Config::prompt_ttl, 5 minutes), in minutes and seconds; amber under 30 seconds.
After an answerAllowed (1d) and denied (1e) stay for 6 seconds and close; Return, Esc, Done or the close button close sooner. Closing then changes nothing: the answer was already sent.
Expired (1f)Stays until dismissed. A new prompt for the same client name closes it first. A server that stops (quit) closes every window without this state.
Size480 × 600 while it asks, the same width as before; the permission list takes the extra height. On an answer it shrinks to the outcome, keeping its top edge: 400 allowed, 280 denied or expired. These fit the content drawn here; when more is needed (a client title that wraps, a wider font) the window grows by what the body overflows, up to 860, and the body scrolls past that. It never runs under the buttons. Each prompt's window is titled PwrRoute — <client>, which is how the resize finds it and what Mission Control shows.

Copy. The title uses the client's title when it sent one, else its name, and wraps: the client chooses it; the mono line is always the name and version as sent, beside “name sent by the client”, because approvals are filed under that name and nothing proves it. The permission labels are Permission::label, in Permission::ALL's order, split after the four every role has. Measurements. Body padding 24, gaps 14; cards 9×12 with a 14px radio, the chosen card --accent-border on --bg-row-active with its name in --accent-bright; the list is a --bg-panel box with 12px glyph tiles (the Roles card's ✓ and —); the footer is 14/24/18 with the countdown in mono 10.5.
