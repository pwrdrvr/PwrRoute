The product surface. Two ordered lists and a default, in the order the router evaluates them: rewrites (every match applies, in order), then handlers (first match wins), then everything else. Rows are dense thread-row style: grip, index, match chips, destination, hits. Selecting a row opens the editor in the right rail; edits save on change and the router hot-reloads rules.json. Family chrome throughout: 40px strip, brand at x=96, settings in its own window.
