1Appearance: Theme, and a theme for each scheme (PWR-22, PWR-23)

PwrRoute has always drawn dark, and its light palette (Contrast and Scale, turn 2) was only a preview. Settings › General was dropped while it had nothing in it; Appearance is what it was waiting for. It goes there rather than in the View menu, because the menu’s rows follow the PwrSuite standard and PwrAgent and PwrGit both keep Theme in General › Appearance. View › Zoom stays in the menu.

1aGeneral comes back for Appearance, second after About and outside the groups, as PwrAgent’s and PwrGit’s General lead their Settings. Theme is System, Dark or Light; System is the default and follows macOS while PwrRoute runs, with no restart. The line under it says what System resolves to now. Increase Contrast stays the OS’s switch; the row says where it is and what it does here. This is what PWR-22 ships.

How it behaves. settings.json keeps appearance.theme ("system", "dark", "light"; left out at System), and with PWR-23 appearance.darkTheme and appearance.lightTheme (left out at Tangerine). It is read before the first window opens, as view.zoom is, so a window never opens in the other scheme first. System follows the OS live: macOS’s effective appearance, Windows’ app mode (AppsUseLightTheme), and the desktop portal’s color-scheme on Linux (no preference is light, as browsers read it). PwrRoute keeps following with every window closed, from the menu bar. Dark and Light also set the app’s own appearance, so the window’s native background, title bar, menus, alerts and file dialogs match the theme rather than the Mac; System clears it. The menu bar icon is a template, which macOS tints for the menu bar, so it follows the menu bar and not the app. On Windows and Linux the tray icon is redrawn in the accent of the scheme in force. The preview switch PWRROUTE_APPEARANCE goes; the screens example keeps --light.
