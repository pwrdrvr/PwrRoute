1Select lists and menus

A select's list is as wide as its field and never narrower. A row's name comes first; the dimmed hint on the right takes what is left and ends in an ellipsis, never past the list's edge. A browser with one or two profiles names them; with three or more the hint is a count, and the profile select beside it lists every one. A list taller than 8½ rows scrolls inside its card, and the half row says there is more.

2Tray dropdown size

The dropdown's window is the card: as tall as what it shows, with nothing below Quit. When the card changes while open (Pause on, the Make default row appears, a new recent link), the window follows it and its top stays under the menu bar. It never runs past the display's visible area (below the menu bar, above the Dock); on a display too short for the card, the header and Quit stay put and the middle scrolls. The pause line names the browser as the reader knows it, never a bundle id.
