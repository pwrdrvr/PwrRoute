1Open in › every browser and its profiles, one click

The Add a rule sheet and the rule editor chose a destination with two selects (browser, then profile) and a separate private-window switch. They become one control: a lane per browser the scan found, the browser’s icon and name, then its profiles as pills (Default first, the browser’s own choice), ending with the browser’s private window in its own word (InPrivate, Incognito, Private window; dashed, with the mask). One pill is on across all lanes; a click is the whole choice: browser, profile and private together. A browser with no private window from a link says so in its lane and has no such pill. The lane the link opened in comes first in the sheet (“where it opened”); the editor keeps the scan’s order with the rule’s own browser first. Pills wrap, so a narrow rail and a browser with eight profiles both fit without a menu. The sheet and the editor draw the same control.
